---
title: "Connect a calendar"
description: "Bring upcoming meetings into Meetspace and start them from one place."
---

Open **Calendar** in the sidebar, add an account, and enable the calendars you want Meetspace to show. Connected events appear in the calendar and timeline.

Meetspace uses calendar events for titles, times, participants, reminders, and meeting links. It does not add a bot to your calls.

## Apple Calendar

Apple Calendar is available on macOS.

1. Choose **Apple Calendar**.
2. Allow Calendar access when macOS asks.
3. Enable the calendars you want to see.

If access was denied, open **Settings → Permissions** in Meetspace. Use **Open** beside Calendar to change the macOS permission.

## Google Calendar

1. Sign in to Meetspace.
2. Choose **Google Calendar**, then finish authorization in your browser.
3. Return to Meetspace and enable the calendars you want to sync.

## Outlook Calendar

1. Sign in to Meetspace.
2. Choose **Outlook Calendar**, then finish authorization in your browser.
3. Return to Meetspace and enable the calendars you want to sync.

<Note>
  Google and Outlook connections are included with Meetspace Pro. Apple Calendar uses the calendar accounts already connected to your Mac.
</Note>

If events are missing, refresh the calendar list, confirm the calendar is enabled, and reconnect any account marked **Reconnect required**.
